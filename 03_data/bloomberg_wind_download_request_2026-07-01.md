## Bottom Line

Download price histories and demand controls for **all 31 policy-screened HS
products**, not only the 10 products already in the processed panel.

The revised theory needs four outcomes:

1. China-source decline from Comtrade.
2. Rest-of-world switching from Comtrade.
3. Total-import compression from Comtrade.
4. Domestic-international price segmentation from Bloomberg/Wind/Fastmarkets,
   SMM, Asian Metal, Argus, Platts, or equivalent terminal-access sources.

Therefore, prices are not side controls. Comparable domestic and international
benchmark pairs are a central outcome for the compression and segmentation
channel.

## Download Rules

- Period: **January 2019 through March 2026**. If the terminal only exports
  through a later date, keep the full raw history; the analysis will trim to
  March 2026.
- Frequency: highest available frequency. Do **not** pre-average in the
  terminal. Daily, weekly, twice-weekly, and monthly series will be converted
  to monthly averages in the repository.
- Export fields: date, identifier, provider, product name, low price, high
  price, midpoint/settlement if available, currency, unit, location, incoterm,
  grade/purity, VAT/tax basis, publication frequency, source terminal, and
  download timestamp.
- Preserve missing values. Do not interpolate raw data.
- For each downloaded assessment, save the metadata page or terminal snapshot
  if possible.
