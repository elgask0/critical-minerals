## Current Scope

The proposal title is:

> When Do Critical-Mineral Export Controls Bind? China's Licensing-Based
> Controls and Heterogeneous Trade Responses

The working research question is:

> Why do formally similar Chinese export-licensing controls generate different
> trade and price disruptions across critical minerals?

The literature corpus must support a proposal, not a finished thesis. Sources
enter the active corpus only when they serve one of four functions:

1. motivate why critical-mineral export controls matter for economic statecraft
   and supply-chain exposure;
2. document China's export-control regime, policy timing, licensing procedure,
   and product scope;
3. explain why products and importers may respond differently to the same
   licensing instrument;
4. justify the proposed empirical design and inference language at the level
   needed for a thesis proposal.

The proposal should not become a source catalogue. The literature review should
synthesize streams of work and cite selectively inside each stream.

## Corpus Admission Rules

| Classification | Meaning | Proposal treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Must-cite | Needed for the proposal argument, core institutional facts, or core method justification | Use in the literature review or data/method sections |
| Should-cite | Useful and likely to appear, but not structurally necessary in every draft | Use if the section has space and the citation does real work |
| Supporting | Contextual, product-specific, or useful for a narrow claim | Use sparingly |
| Optional | Useful only if a later subsection needs the specific caveat | Defer unless needed |
| Deferred | Relevant for the full thesis or a later empirical extension, but not part of the proposal baseline | Do not cite in the baseline proposal except to explain future expansion |

Official documents are evidence for policy design and timing. They are not
theory. Academic sources support mechanisms, methods, and interpretation.
Market and institutional reports support product facts and data interpretation,
not causal identification by themselves.

- Do not cite official approval statements as proof that controls were
  non-binding. Use them only to show that the regimes were licensing systems
  rather than automatic bans.
- Do not cite the Ga/Ge or graphite license-guide folders until the guide PDFs
  are recaptured. The current local `source.pdf` files are failed-download
  placeholders, so the control announcements and PRC law/regulation should carry
  the licensing-procedure claims for now.

  causal trade effects. Use them to motivate exposure and heterogeneity.

  design is explicitly expanded.
