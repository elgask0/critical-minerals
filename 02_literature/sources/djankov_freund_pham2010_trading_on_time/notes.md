# Notes

## Project Use

Core source for administrative delay as a trade cost.

## Can Support

Administrative and logistical delays reduce trade. The paper estimates that each additional day a product is delayed before shipment reduces trade by more than 1 percent and is equivalent to increasing distance from trading partners.

## Cannot Support

Measures general pre-shipment delays, not dual-use export licensing. Use as general evidence that administrative time costs affect trade.
