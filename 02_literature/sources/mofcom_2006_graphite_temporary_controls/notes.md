page confirms that the page loads the 2006 announcement text but the table after

broken embedded image. The local PDF capture has the same limitation: it
preserves the surrounding official text, dates, and licensing paragraph, but not

as secondary verification unless the original table image is recovered from an
official cache or a clear official screenshot.
