# Notes

## Project Use

Core source for the policy-uncertainty component of the licensing wedge.

## Can Support

Trade policy uncertainty affects firm entry, trade, prices, and welfare. In the China-U.S. context, reduced uncertainty after China's WTO accession explains a substantial share of export growth and consumer gains.

## Cannot Support

The source studies tariff-policy uncertainty and WTO accession, not Chinese export licensing. Use for the general uncertainty channel.
