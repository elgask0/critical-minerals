# Notes

## Project Use

Optional source for commodity-price benchmark context and World Bank commodity-market reporting conventions.

## Can Support

The report presents commodity price developments, forecasts, and benchmark definitions across energy, agriculture, fertilizers, metals, and minerals. It is useful mainly as a discipline check for how commodity-price series are defined and reported.

## Cannot Support

This is not the same as a mineral-specific Bloomberg/Wind methodology sheet. It cannot validate price series for gallium, germanium, graphite, or antimony grades.
