# Notes

## Project Use

Supporting source for price volatility responses to trade-policy announcements.

## Can Support

Trade policy announcements can increase global commodity price volatility. The effect is observed for export restrictions and import liberalization measures and is stronger when markets are tight and stocks are low.

## Cannot Support

Food commodity setting, not critical minerals. Use for announcement/volatility logic only.
