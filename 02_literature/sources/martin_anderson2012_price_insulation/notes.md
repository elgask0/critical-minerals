# Notes

## Project Use

Core source for domestic-international price wedge and price-insulation logic.

## Can Support

Trade policy changes, especially export restrictions, can insulate domestic markets from international price spikes while shifting pressure onto world markets. The paper frames export restrictions as part of a collective-action problem in commodity markets.

## Cannot Support

The setting is agricultural commodities, not critical minerals. Use for price-wedge logic, not for mineral-specific magnitudes.
