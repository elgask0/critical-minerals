# Notes

## Project Use

Core source for the delay/time-cost component of the licensing wedge.

## Can Support

Time in transit is economically costly. The paper estimates that each day in transit is equivalent to an ad valorem tariff of roughly 0.6 to 2.1 percent, with especially high sensitivity for parts and components trade.

## Cannot Support

Shipping-time evidence, not export-license processing evidence. Use to justify why delays from licensing can matter economically.
