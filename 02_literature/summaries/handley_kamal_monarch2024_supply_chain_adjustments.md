## Project Role

## Main Argument

Using confidential firm trade data from the 2018-2019 U.S. trade war, the paper shows that import declines after tariff shocks were driven by firm exit, discontinued buyer-supplier relationships, and reduced entry into importing. It also finds that products with imports concentrated in fewer suppliers had smaller import-growth declines.
