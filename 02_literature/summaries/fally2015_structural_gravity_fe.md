## Main Argument

Structural gravity with multilateral resistance terms and reduced-form gravity with exporter and importer fixed effects are closely connected when adding-up constraints are imposed. The paper shows that Poisson PML with fixed effects can be interpreted in structural-gravity terms under the relevant constraints.
