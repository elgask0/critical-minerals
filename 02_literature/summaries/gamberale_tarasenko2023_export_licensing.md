## Main Argument

Export licensing is a procedure that requires prior authorization before exportation and can be governed by automatic or non-automatic rules. The paper reviews existing WTO rules and explains why export licensing can matter as a trade-restrictive administrative layer, especially when procedures are burdensome, opaque, or discretionary.
