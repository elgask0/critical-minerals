Research planning, argument design, evidence tracking, data documentation,
methodology, and revision notes live here.

Canonical files:

- `Post-Defense Thesis Plan.md`: current source of truth after the proposal
  defense; use this for the active model hierarchy, baseline timing,
  preliminary result map, and next steps.
- `Thesis Design.md`: research question, empirical architecture, current
  results, and minimum-success criteria. It now follows the post-defense
  pressure-mechanism design.
- `Theoretical Framework Lock.md`: final proposal-stage theoretical model,
  hypotheses, empirical mapping, and interpretation rules.
- `Empirical Strategy Lock.md`: minimum proposal-stage empirical design,
  estimand, timing, inference language, and robustness boundaries.
- `Licensing Wedge Framework.md`: older term-paper reduced-form model and
  result alignment after the 2019-01--2026-03 panel rerun. Retain it for
  provenance, not as the current thesis backbone.
- `Data.md`: authoritative data inventory and required external variables.
- `Methodology.md`: operational estimator specifications.
- `MOFCOM Deep Research Prompt.md`: official-document collection protocol.
- `Plan.md`: complete thesis roadmap.

`Motivation.md`, `Differential Effectiveness.md`, and older term-paper
planning notes are retained for provenance. Do not use them to decide the
current thesis claim unless the claim is reproduced in the pressure-design
pipeline.
