# Policy Chronology Audit

This audit checks the project chronology in `01_notes/Official Documents.md`
against the local official-source folders, `02_literature/references.bib`, and
live official MOFCOM pages. It is a chronology and coding-control note, not the
proposal prose.

## Bottom Line

The core treatment chronology is internally consistent. The main proposal
baseline should continue to treat the country-neutral 2023-2024 licensing
episodes as the clean policy sequence:

| Episode | Announcement | Announced | Effective | Baseline coding implication |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Gallium/germanium | MOFCOM-GAC No. 23/2023 | 2023-07-03 | 2023-08-01 | New country-neutral licensing treatment |
| Graphite | MOFCOM-GAC No. 39/2023 | 2023-10-20 | 2023-12-01 | Adjustment of pre-existing graphite controls; No. 50/2006 repealed |
| Antimony | MOFCOM-GAC No. 33/2024 | 2024-08-15 | 2024-09-15 | New country-neutral licensing treatment for antimony value-chain products |

For importer-arrival outcomes, the working empirical interpretation should keep
the one-month shipping/reporting lag already used in the project notes:
Ga/Ge from September 2023, graphite from January 2024, and antimony from October
2024.

## Coding Decisions For The Proposal

- Keep No. 23/2023, No. 39/2023, and No. 33/2024 as the main proposal
  chronology because they are country-neutral licensing measures and already
  align with the available Comtrade treatment sample.
- Treat No. 46/2024 and No. 72/2025 as U.S.-destination policy context unless a
  separate destination-specific design is added.
- Treat No. 10/2025 and No. 18/2025 as thesis expansion candidates. They are
  official and locally captured, but their HS product crosswalk still needs to
  be coded before they can enter a clean empirical panel.
- Do not code Announcement No. 58/2025 as an implemented treatment in the
  baseline chronology. Announcement No. 70/2025 suspended Announcements 55-58
  and 61-62 before their scheduled implementation.
- Use public MOFCOM license statements only as evidence that the regimes
  operated through licensing rather than automatic blanket bans. They do not
  report approval rates, denial rates, processing times, or destination-level
  license outcomes.

## Remaining Chronology Work

- Verify the exact item table in No. 50/2006 before citing detailed predecessor
  graphite scope.
- Complete HS-level treatment crosswalks for the 2025 expansion episodes:
  W/Te/Bi/Mo/In and medium/heavy rare earths.
- Keep the U.S.-destination measures out of baseline global treatment coding
  unless the empirical design explicitly adds destination interactions.
- When drafting the proposal background, cite the official announcement pages
  directly rather than relying on secondary descriptions of the measures.
