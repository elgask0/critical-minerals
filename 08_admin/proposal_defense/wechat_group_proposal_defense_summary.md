# WeChat Group Notes: Proposal Defense

This note summarizes the operational guidance from the WeChat conversation provided by the user.

## What The Proposal Must Prove

The proposal defense is used to prove that the student has:

- a good research idea;
- a feasible research plan;
- potential contribution to the literature;
- supervisor confirmation that the project can continue.

If the defense committee is convinced, the student can continue the research and write the thesis based on it.

## Application Process

- If the proposal is finished, or will be finished before the relevant internal deadline, and the supervisor has confirmed feasibility, send a message to Ms Du and apply for defense.
- Keep both the supervisor and Ms Du informed about progress.
- For Mario: supervisor approval has already been obtained according to the user.

## Required Deliverables Mentioned

- Written thesis proposal.
- Slides.
- The two official documents in the red-box notice:
  - proposal defense review form;
  - remuneration/signature form.

## Presentation

- Presentation length: 5-7 minutes.
- Follow the committee's instruction if they give a specific time.

## Suggested Proposal Sections

The WeChat guidance recommended at least:

1. Research background and research question(s).
2. Literature review and potential contribution.
3. Research method and structure of the thesis.
   - Method.
   - Regression equations.
   - Data.
   - Key variables.
   - Summary statistics.
   - Possible results.
4. Research plan and time schedule.
5. References.

The message says references should include more than 20 papers. The official detailed outline says at least 30, so the working target should be 30+.

## Naming / Identity Note

The group asked students to include student number and name in the WeChat group nickname.
