## Pre-Defense Requirements

- The student must write the opening report according to academic norms and the ZJU master thesis format.
- The draft must be reviewed and approved by the supervisor.
- The supervisor should sign the proposal cover before the student participates in the defense.
- If the student cannot attend, they must ask the supervisor and graduate office for leave in advance.

## Materials To Bring

1. Printed opening report copies.
   - Number of copies should match the number of committee members.
2. `浙江大学经济学院攻读硕士学位研究生开题报告审核表`.
   - Print double-sided.
   - One copy.
3. `浙江大学研究生学位论文评阅、答辩酬金表`.
   - One copy.
   - Fill student name and committee-member names.
   - Do not change payment amounts.
   - Put the committee chair in the 300 RMB row.
4. Slides / PPT if needed.
   - Presentation length: 5-7 minutes.
   - Bring a USB copy if presenting in person.

## Review Form Instructions

- Fill basic information completely.
- The proposed title must exactly match the title entered in the Graduate Education Management New System.
- `开题报告记录` should record actual committee questions and student answers.
- `记录人` is signed by the student assisting with the record.
- `考评小组意见` should combine committee comments and suggestions; signed by the committee chair.
- After the defense, revise the proposal and literature review according to committee comments.
- The supervisor fills and signs `指导教师意见` after student revisions.
- `考评小组成员` should list committee members and collect each committee member's signature.
- Committee titles can be checked on the School website under organization/personnel/faculty team.

## After Defense

- Upload the revised opening report PDF and proposal information to the Graduate Education Management New System:
  - 培养 -> 培养过程 -> 开题报告
- Ask the supervisor to approve the system submission.
- Submit signed paper materials according to the current office/class monitor instruction.
- The 2026 spring notice says full-time students should submit the signed review form and remuneration form through the class monitor by May 12 to Econ 319, Graduate Office.
- After college confirmation in the system, the thesis title cannot be changed.

## Warnings

- Failure to submit paper materials or system confirmation on time may delay the thesis process or block thesis review.
- The notice also states that full-time, international, and part-time students should complete a midterm progress report three months before pre-defense.
- It says 25级硕士 should complete the midterm progress report before October 25, 2025. Because the date is already in the past relative to June 26, 2026, confirm whether this applies to the current cohort or whether a later year is intended.
