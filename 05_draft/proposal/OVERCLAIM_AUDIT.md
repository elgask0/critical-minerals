# Overclaim Audit

## Summary

The proposal defines binding as an observed market response in trade or price data. It does not claim to observe license denials, approval probabilities, coercive success, welfare effects, or clean causal timing. The quarterly PPML baseline is the preferred empirical object, monthly models are robustness and timing diagnostics, and product interpretation carries the thesis.

## Claim Discipline

| Risk area | Current treatment |
|---|---|
| License denial | Mentioned only as an object the thesis does not identify. |
| Approval probability and processing delays | Explicitly excluded from the empirical definition of binding. |
| Welfare loss | Not estimated; appears only as a non claim. |
| Coercive success | Not estimated; appears only as a non claim or literature caveat. |
| Clean causal timing | Event studies and placebos are timing diagnostics only. |
| Pooled estimates | Used only as summary evidence because pooled pretrends fail. |
| Monthly diagnostics | Treated as robustness and timing diagnostics only. Missing or failed diagnostics are unavailable evidence, not passing checks. |
| Price wedges | Treated as diagnostic evidence, not proof of licensing denial, compression, or welfare effects. |
| Tungstates/APT | Classified as weak, null, or unresolved. |
| Compression | Interpreted as product specific, mainly graphite, not a general thesis result. |

## Product Interpretation Check

| Product | Current interpretation | Overclaim control |
|---|---|---|
| Natural graphite | Moderate average decline; clear switching and compression evidence in the quarterly mechanism models; no positive price segmentation. | Price evidence is not treated as segmentation. |
| Antimony metal | Strongest observed disruption with switching and price segmentation; timing and placebo caveat. | Compression is not supported; timing and placebo caveats remain visible. |
| Bismuth metal | Strong average decline and strong price segmentation; weaker quantity mechanisms. | Quantity mechanisms are described as weaker or imprecise. |
| Tungstates/APT | Weak, null, or unresolved; bad timing and placebo diagnostics. | Not treated as a positive mechanism case despite legal coverage. |

## Final Negative Checks

- No clean causal treatment effect claim is introduced.
- No uniform collapse claim is introduced.
- No license denial, welfare loss, or coercive success estimate is introduced.
- No old HS 282580 result appears in the active proposal.
- No old 10 product feasibility table appears in the active proposal.
- No event study figure is used as main evidence.
